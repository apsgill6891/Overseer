Meet the three types of agents

Think of this as one manager, one workflow lead, and a team of specialists. Review exactly what each may do.

1

ORCHESTRATION AGENT · THE MANAGER

Fulfillment Coordinator

Chooses which workflow is needed, reacts when conditions change, pauses unsafe work, and asks you for decisions.

May: choose workflows, replan, pause, request approval
May not: bypass a rule or approve its own exception
2

GOAL AGENT · THE WORKFLOW LEAD

Order Planning Lead

Makes sure every required check is completed, compares valid plans, and recommends the most defensible option.

May: request specialist checks and recommend a plan
May not: change inventory or create a shipment directly
3

TASK AGENTS · THE SPECIALISTS

Fact-checking team

Each specialist answers one narrow question. Their calculations are deterministic and independently recorded.

Why are specialist checks locked?Removing a safety, inventory, cost, or promise check could create an invalid plan. You control priorities and authority—not whether evidence is collected.

Set the team’s limits

The recommended values match the selected outcome. Tightening a limit sends more decisions to you; loosening it permits more automatic work.
